Eddy — Privacy Policy

Effective October 8, 2026

Eddy is a trip-planning app for river trips. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: an account is optional. Without one, your trips stay on your device. If you create an account or share a trip, we store only what is described below.

What we collect

Eddy has no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and it works fully without an account. We do not receive your trips, names, notes, costs or anything else you enter, with one exception you control: if you tap Share with crew on a trip, a copy of that trip (its crew names, itinerary, meals, gear, costs and notes) is stored on a server so your crew's devices can stay in sync. Only that trip is sent, and only after you choose to share it.

Accounts (optional)

You can create an account with your email address, or sign in with Google or Apple. We store your email address (and, if the provider shares it, your name) to run your account, and nothing else about you. When you are signed in, your trips are backed up to our server under your account so they can sync across your devices. They are visible only to you, and to anyone you give a trip's join code. You can sign out at any time, and Profile → Account → Delete my account permanently deletes your account, your backed-up trips and any trips you shared. Sign-in is provided by Supabase; if you use Google or Apple they handle that step under their own privacy policies and tell us only your email and name.

Feedback

If you send feedback from Profile → Feedback, we store the message you write, the date, the app version and platform, the screen and river you were looking at, and a random per-device identifier used to limit abuse. If you are signed in we link it to your account; if you tick You can email me about this we also store that email address so we can reply. Please do not include sensitive information in feedback. We use feedback only to fix problems and decide what to build, and it is not shared. When you delete your account, your feedback is kept without any link to you and your contact email is removed.

Display names

You choose the name other people see on a shared trip: a first name, a nickname or a full name. It is optional to use your real name, and you can change it at any time under Profile → Account. We do not ask for your last name, phone number or contacts.

Location and maps. If you tap My location on the topo map, the app reads your device's location once to drop a pin there. That position, and any pins you drop, stay on your device; they are not sent to us or to anyone else unless you export them yourself. The topo map's terrain comes from the Amazon Web Services open-data copy of USGS elevation, and the optional Topo and Satellite layers from the USGS National Map. Like any website, those servers see your IP address and which map squares you load (that is, the area you are looking at). Terrain you download for offline use is stored only on your device.

Acceptable use

Shared trips are private, invite-only workspaces for planning a river trip with people you know. Do not post content that is unlawful, harassing, hateful or sexually explicit, and do not use a shared trip to send spam. The trip owner can remove any member and reset the invite link at any time. To report a problem, use Report a problem on the People screen or Profile → About → Support & feedback. We will remove content or trips that break these rules and may close accounts that repeatedly do.

Shared trips

A shared trip is stored on our server under a private trip identifier. People get in through an invite link or a 16-character invite code; the server keeps only a one-way hash of the code. When someone joins, they enter a display name, which is stored with the trip and shown to the other members, together with their role (owner, can edit, or view only) and a short activity log of what changed and who changed it. Each device is identified by a random secret that the server keeps only as a hash; it is not linked to your email, contact list or location. The trip owner can change members' roles, remove people and reset the invite link. Choosing Stop sharing in the app deletes the server copy immediately; people who already synced keep the copy on their own device. Members can Leave trip at any time. Server hosting is provided by Supabase; the usual short-lived network and security logs kept by a hosting provider are outside our control.

Where your data lives

Trips and rivers you create are stored locally on your device. If you delete the app, or choose Erase all data in the app, the local copy is removed (a trip you shared stays on the server until its owner chooses Stop sharing). We cannot recover local data for you, so use General → About → Back up all trips to save a copy.

Backups and sharing

When you back up your trips or share a trip summary, the app hands the file or text to the iOS share sheet. Where it goes (Files, iCloud Drive, Messages, Mail and so on) is your choice and is governed by that service's own policies.

Notifications

If you turn on trip reminders, the app asks permission and schedules local notifications on your device before your launch date. They are generated on the device; no notification service or server is involved. You can turn them off in the app or in iOS Settings.

Third parties

The app contains no third-party analytics, advertising or tracking software. It contacts our server only for sign-in, for trips backed up to your account, and for trips you have shared or joined. Links to this policy and to our support page open in your web browser.

Children

Eddy is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Safety information

Medical, rescue and river information in the app is general reference material and may be out of date or incomplete. It is not medical advice. Always confirm regulations, permits, flows and conditions with the managing agency, and carry appropriate emergency communication.
